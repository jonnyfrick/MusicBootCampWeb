Sound test

The first six buttons play 440 Hz for 2 seconds. A clean tone is a plain beep; a broken one rattles, warbles or has a second tone.

The app's piano without a worklet: a piano-like tone from a buffer, played by the browser at another pitch, retuned and faded out as the app does with its samples.

Recording: 2 seconds from the microphone (play or sing something), then played back with the microphone closed. A clean recording sounds like what you played.
